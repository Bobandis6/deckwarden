2. **The band.** `page.tsx` moves the container from `<main>` onto each band, so the hero section is full-bleed with its background and the rest sits in `max-w-wide`. Inside the band: from `lg` a split (text left, the trio right), stacked below; the H1 on `.type-hero` with per-tier `max-width` chosen so Literata and its fallback wrap alike (U5's method); the sentence as one `<p>`; the stage glow as the band's background gradient, its center under the cards.
3. **The trio.** `HeroFan` keeps its name, data and `priority`; the `.home-fan` rule becomes a three-column grid of upright cards with explicit `aspect-ratio`, the tinted hero shadow, no rotation, no lift. The test's geometry pins move to the grid.
4. **The doors row** under the band: left, the `hero` Button "Build a deck" with the arrow, full width of its column, and a ghost "Browse commanders" with an arrow; right, `nav[aria-label="Choose your game"]` with the two accent-field tiles (serif name on `.type-section`, the format line on `.type-label-sm`, the arrow; a 2 px lift on settle-120 `motion-safe:`, the game ring on focus). The tiles' field is the accent at about 14 % over the card tone in oklab, measured to 4.5:1 for the name in both themes.
5. **The gallery.** `GameCard` grows a `headline` prop: the Magic region renders its h2 on `.type-headline` with the text "Find your next commander."; the Magic button row stays under the gallery (Build a deck · Browse commanders · Search cards · Surprise me, hrefs byte-identical). `MagicShelf` takes the `normal` rendition (`shelves.ts`), the grid `repeat(3, minmax(0, 1fr))` and `lg:repeat(6, …)`, captions `text-sm` with `line-clamp-2` and a two-line floor so rows align. The One Piece field keeps its shape with the same grid; the posture line moves from 11 px to `.type-meta-multiline`.
6. **Rhythm.** The band and the doors row replace the first hairline; the hairlines between the later sections stay (D6). Nothing else on the page changes.
