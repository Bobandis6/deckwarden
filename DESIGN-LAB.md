### B2. shadcn lens (the v4 registry against `src/components/ui/`)

Evidence: `.claude/design-lab/lenses/shadcn.md` (findings F1 to F9, the eight-style button matrix, four directions, the family sketch, pattern ideas, palettes) and `lenses/button-candidates.ts` (today, A, D, C, B as literal class recipes; every candidate passes `src/components/ui/button.test.tsx` per the lens; the main session verified zero em or en dashes and the F2 and F4 numbers).
