  candidates here.

**The four parts:**
1. **IO** — recommended: an ADDITIVE `tournaments` block on the combos route's response, computed

3. **`CUT_WEIGHTS` rebalance**, sum stays 1 — starting proposal `popularity .30 / tournaments .25

4. **Test surface** — `cuts.test.ts` grows present/absent/low/high cases plus the interplay pins
