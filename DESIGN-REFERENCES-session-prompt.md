## The ask, deepened

Four things remain from the six-site study, and the study's reasoning for each still stands: the card is the object, the numbers are honest, the chrome gets out of the way.

1. **CARD-TILT** (idea 3): the three home hero cards, the hub hero and the editor's leader card turn a few degrees toward the pointer, with their grounded shadow moving the other way. The affordance for the one click each surface wants. Fine pointers only.
2. **ROLLING-NUMBERS** (idea 4): where a number is the feedback for an action (the price in the fit and swap sheets, the bracket number, the deck total), its digits roll to the new value instead of jumping. The count-up hook exists; this gives it digits and two more homes.
3. **SEARCH-EXAMPLES** (idea 5): under the `/cards` box before any query and in the header popup's empty state, a "Try" line of real example links that teach what the search accepts (names, One Piece ids). Zero motion.
4. **HUB-TO-EDITOR** (idea 1, phase 2; LATER row 210's second half): the hub hero morphs into the editor's leader card on "Build with this commander". Gated on one check, written below, that the first session on it must do before any wrapping.

Deeper means grounded and verified: every number read in a browser against a recorded baseline, every string through the copy policy, every motion on the four curves. Not more features.

## Build 1: CARD-TILT (idea 3)

Reference: Aceternity "3D Card Effect", https://ui.aceternity.com/components/3d-card-effect. Borrow only the pointer-following rotation; not the floating child layers, not a glare (it reads as foil and would lie on non-foil printings).

1. **One small module**, e.g. `src/lib/motion/tilt.ts`: a `tiltFor(pointer, rect, maxDeg)` pure function (x and y in degrees from the pointer's position over the element, clamped) and an `attachTilt(el, { maxDeg })` that listens to `pointermove` / `pointerleave` on the element and writes `--tilt-x`, `--tilt-y` and `--tilt-dx`, `--tilt-dy` (the shadow's offset, against the tilt, up to 4 px) as inline custom properties. No React state, no re-render, no `requestAnimationFrame` loop (one write per event is enough; the CSS transition does the smoothing). Fine pointers only: attach nothing when `matchMedia("(pointer: fine)")` is false or `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is true. Unit tests for the math and the gates.
2. **The CSS**, in `globals.css` beside the stage rules: a `.card-tilt` recipe with `transform: rotateX(var(--tilt-x, 0deg)) rotateY(var(--tilt-y, 0deg))` composed AFTER whatever the element already has (the hero's outer cells keep their 9° on the CELL; the tilt goes on the card inside the cell, so the artist-line rule holds), `transition: transform 120ms var(--ease-settle-120), box-shadow 120ms var(--ease-settle-120)` under `motion-safe:` and `pointer-fine:`, and the existing grounded shadow reading the `--tilt-d*` offsets. Transforms and box-shadow only: no filter, or `tokens.test.ts` fails.
3. **Three homes, in this order, each measured:** the three home hero cards (max 6°; the tilt attaches only after the entrance ends, `animationend` on the last card or the gate attribute leaving, so the two never fight; the hero gate's keyframes are a single `0s` animation, read `card-entrance.ts` first), the hub hero on `/c/` and `/l/` (max 6°; it is the morph's source, so the tilt must be at rest when the click lands: reset on `pointerdown`), the editor's leader card (max 4°: a working surface, never list rows; the stepper rows and the search rows get nothing).
4. **Done when:** a real pointer in headless Chrome moves across each card and `getComputedStyle(card).transform` reads a matrix3d with the expected angle at the edges and identity at rest; the home trio's artist and © strips stay uncovered at the maximum tilt (reuse the strip-point check from LATER row 298's evidence); the hub hero morph still pairs on a local production build (the view-transition wrapper reads the group animation); reduced motion and a coarse pointer attach nothing (the Simulator or an emulated touch device); `pnpm check` green; the REDESIGN.md addendum and LATER rows for what is left (the phone's device-motion tilt is out: iOS needs a permission tap; Design Spells "Card preview tilts as you move your phone in Craft" is the reference not to copy).

## Build 2: ROLLING-NUMBERS (idea 4)

Reference: Aceternity "Speed Counter", https://ui.aceternity.com/interactions/speed-counter, for the one idea in it: the digits move and nothing else does. Not the sideways smear. 24fps's stat band is the static typographic form.

1. **`RollingNumber`**, e.g. `src/components/ui/rolling-number.tsx`: renders a number as fixed-width tabular digit columns (`tabular-nums`, one `span` per digit with the font's digit height), each column translating vertically from the old digit to the new on settle-250 over 250 ms, with currency, separators and the decimal point standing still. It takes the value and the formatter (`formatUsd` for prices, plain for counts), and the same `threshold` rule as `useCountUp`: a delta at or under the threshold jumps. Under reduced motion it renders the plain text. Build it on `useCountUp`'s sampling or a per-digit transform; either way the displayed number always lands on the exact value and is announced once (`aria-live="polite"` on a visually hidden plain copy, the columns `aria-hidden`), never per digit.
2. **Three homes:** the fit sheet's and the target fit sheet's total when a swap lands (the price is the point of those sheets; thresholds of 0 there, every change rolls); the swap sheet's price delta if it shows one; the bracket number in the bracket line when the bracket changes (the line's text is the adapter's; the number inside it is what rolls, so split it at the adapter boundary without moving a string); the deck total in `deck-list-pane.tsx`, replacing the plain `useCountUp` render under today's threshold (±1 jumps, above 5 rolls).
3. **Done when:** RTL tests pin the digit columns, the jump path, the reduced-motion text and the single announcement; `copy.test.ts` and the bracket line's pins hold (no string moved); the editor's status slot keeps its fixed width (U3a: the save slot and the count never shift layout; measure the slot's width before and after at 390 and 1440); a real swap in the fit sheet on a local production build rolls the total once; `pnpm check` green; REDESIGN.md and LATER.
